Your data stays yours

Coinly is built offline-first. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose a cloud feature. Read the Privacy Policy.

  • Stored on your device. Your transactions live in the app, not on our servers.
  • No ads, no analytics. Nothing tracks what you do in the app.
  • Backups in your own Drive. Optional, and Coinly can only see the files it creates.
  • App lock. Protect Coinly with your fingerprint, face or passcode.
