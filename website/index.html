Coinly

A private, offline-first budget and expense tracker for Android.

Coinly helps you record income and expenses, set monthly budgets, track debts and savings goals, and see where your money goes. Your financial records are stored on your own device.

Features

  • Income and expense tracking across multiple accounts
  • Monthly and category budgets with charts and insights
  • Recurring payments, bill reminders, debts and savings goals
  • Receipt scanning, with text read on your device
  • An optional AI assistant that turns messages like "coffee 150" into draft transactions
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive
  • App lock with your device's biometrics or passcode
Your data, your device.

Coinly does not run ads or analytics. Data only leaves your phone when you choose to use cloud features such as the AI assistant or Google Drive backup. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
